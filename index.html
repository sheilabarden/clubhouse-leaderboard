Like a coach you never forget, how we speak is memorable. Every callout has a name so it can be announced, repeated, and owned.

How callouts are given

Coach's Callout

Your coach sees it on the floor or at the pre-shift huddle. Every huddle includes at least one callout — non-negotiable.

Peer Nomination

Fill out a Peer Callout card or the digital form and drop it in the Callout Box. Anyone can nominate anyone — and teammate nominations count double toward All-Star & Playmaker.

Athlete Recognition

A guest compliment, an online review, or a comment card that names you counts. Your coach logs it the same day.

The callout is only half of it. The other half is saying it out loud in front of the team. Recognition that stays quiet is a missed swing.
